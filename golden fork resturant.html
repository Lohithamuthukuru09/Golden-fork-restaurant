Our Story

Crafted with love, served with warmth

Founded in 2010, The Golden Fork began as a small family café with one dream — to bring people together over honest, delicious food.

Today, we're proud to serve handcrafted dishes made from fresh, locally-sourced ingredients, in a space that feels like home.

Our Fruit Promise 🌿

At The Golden Fork, we care about your health beyond your meal. Every guest receives a fresh, seasonal fruit to take home while leaving — a small gift from our family to yours, because good health begins with small habits.
